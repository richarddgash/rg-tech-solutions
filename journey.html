The Journey

Five stages, one continuous path

Not five separate services — one engagement that moves your team from a queue nobody trusts to a service that improves itself.

1

Triage

Everything's "Moderate" or "Low"→Every ticket scored the same way, every time

It starts with a queue nobody trusts. Security first, then real business impact, then SLA — not whoever's shouting loudest. That's the whole framework, run consistently against every ticket.

159
tickets mis-prioritised as low-urgency in our own proof-of-concept run — including a broken phishing-report tool and a lost company device
Source: RG Tech Solutions ticket triage test, 1,133 tickets
2

Resolve

The backlog just sits there, re-sorted→It actually gets cleared

Once priority is honest, the easy wins clear fast — and what's genuinely hard gets a person's full attention instead of a share of everyone's. That split matters more every year: the simple stuff increasingly resolves itself, so what's left needs real judgement.

18% slower
industry-wide resolution times last year, even as first response got faster — AI is absorbing the easy tickets, leaving harder ones for people
Source: Freshworks Customer Service Benchmark Report 2026
3

Knowledge

Every fix starts from scratch→Nobody solves the same problem twice

Every fix becomes an article — scattered documentation consolidated, common issues written up in your own voice — so your team stops re-solving the same problem and your people can solve the easy ones themselves.

54.9%
of what an AI copilot actually does for agents is surface the right knowledge article — the single biggest use case, ahead of drafting or tone
Source: Freshworks Customer Service Benchmark Report 2026
4

Automate

Every request needs a person→Most of them stop reaching one

The requests that don't need a person anymore, stop reaching one — password resets, status checks, routine approvals. We scope what should be owned end-to-end, not just bolted onto the existing process, and check the automations are actually feasible before promising them.

83.1%
average ticket deflection rate reported once AI agents are properly scoped to a workflow, not just layered on top of one
Source: Freshworks Customer Service Benchmark Report 2026
5

Improve

A dashboard that says everything's fine→Customers who'll tell you when it isn't

You stop guessing whether it's working. Experience surveys tell you why people scored things the way they did, not just the score — so the next thing to fix gets found before anyone has to complain about it.

52.7%
of organisations say customer service now decides who a customer chooses over the competition — a growth lever, not a cost centre
Source: Freshworks Customer Service Benchmark Report 2026
